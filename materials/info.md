# MIRABO Biotechnology
* MIRABO Biotechnology Limited is a Hong Kong–based synthetic biology company pioneering AI-integrated platforms to create next-generation bioactive ingredients and medical materials for the personal-care and healthcare industries.
* Its subsidiary, Shanghai MIRABO Biotechnology, is an AI biotech company advancing protein in-silico prediction, protein engineering, and bioprocess modeling and engineering through its proprietary MiRAiQ platform. By combining AI-driven modeling with experimental validation, Shanghai MIRABO improves the precision, speed, and scalability of biologics R&D from molecular design to process optimization.
